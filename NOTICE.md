# Data and software notices

AquaNavAI derives research artifacts from United States government marine data,
including NOAA/NCEI ETOPO and CUDEM products, NOAA/NCEP RTOFS forecasts, and
NOAA/NDBC observations. Each released scenario includes exact citations,
retrieval metadata, transformations, and checksums in its provenance file.

Map rendering uses MapLibre GL JS. Data attribution remains visible in the user
interface. AquaNavAI outputs are research products and are not NOAA products or
official navigation guidance.
