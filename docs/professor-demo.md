# Professor demonstration sequence

1. Frame the question: how do forecast currents affect feasible coastal routes and a reference-ASV energy proxy?
2. Open provenance and identify CUDEM, the 2026-07-12 00Z RTOFS product, NDBC context, checksums, and limitations.
3. Select a shelf mission or click two offshore points; explain 500 m snapping and the 5 m mask.
4. Compare white dashed shortest, cyan fastest, green lowest-modelled-energy, and amber balanced routes. Explain legitimate overlap.
5. Play the ASV and demonstrate measured pixel/geographic movement, pause/resume, 1x/5x/10x, seek, and reset.
6. Change cycle and mission while explaining worker cancellation and deterministic recomputation.
7. State that propulsion energy is modelled, RTOFS is model output, and NDBC values are observations.
8. Show one-command build, schema/checksum validation, cross-language routing tests, and offline browser verification.
9. Close with pinned ENC constraints, calibrated vessel/simulator labels, waves, uncertainty, and only then a justified learned surrogate.
