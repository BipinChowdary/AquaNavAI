# Professor demonstration sequence

1. Frame the question: can forecast currents change a feasible coastal route or its modelled energy for a reference ASV?
2. Open **Data provenance** and identify CUDEM, the 2026-07-12 00Z RTOFS cycle, NDBC context, checksums, and known limits.
3. Select a verified shelf mission or click two offshore points; explain 500 m cell snapping and the 5 m minimum-depth mask.
4. Compare the white distance Dijkstra path with the teal environmental A* path and inspect paired metrics.
5. Select **Play ASV**, pause/resume, change speed, and describe the geodesic progress interpolation.
6. Move the forecast timeline and recalculate in the worker to show a different valid-time current field.
7. State explicitly that propulsion energy is a modelled proxy, RTOFS currents are model output, and NDBC values are observations.
8. Show the one-command build, schema/checksum validation, cross-language route test, and offline browser test.
9. Close with the next experiment: calibrated vessel/simulator energy labels, more forecast cycles/seasons, uncertainty, and then—only if justified—a learned surrogate.
